✈️ Flight Price & Airline Analysis – Key Insights:

Key Insights:

Airline affects ticket prices: Different airlines have different average ticket prices.

Stops affect prices: Non-stop and connecting flights have different pricing patterns.

Expensive routes: Some source-to-destination routes have much higher average prices.

Flight duration: Longer journeys can have higher ticket prices.

Departure time: Prices change based on the time of departure.

Popular routes: Routes with more flight options may show different price patterns.

Price variation: Ticket prices range from low to very high depending on the flight details.

Destination matters: Flights to some destinations are more expensive than others.

Source city matters: The starting city also shows differences in average ticket prices.

Overall finding: Flight prices depend on multiple factors rather than a single factor.

Final Conclusion:
The analysis shows that airline, route, number of stops, duration, source, destination, and departure time all play an important role in flight pricing.
